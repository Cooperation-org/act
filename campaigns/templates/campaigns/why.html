What this platform does

Named people put their name on every campaign

Before a campaign is listed, people who know the organizer record testimonials — many on video — and sign them as LinkedClaims. Each testimonial links to the person who gave it, and to the people who vouch for them.

The money route is clear

Donations are received by Civic Works, a 501(c)(3), which issues tax receipts and holds full discretion and control over donated funds. Campaigns post receipts for what is spent, as updates.

Money is one of many ways in

Every campaign lists work you can do with the people on the ground: mentoring and English practice, hiring for remote contracts, hosting an event, joining a live AMA.
